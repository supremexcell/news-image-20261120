## 1. Karhutla — Indonesia burns for four months (hero)
- EU Copernicus CAMS, via Reuters 19 Nov / Bloomberg Technoz 20 Nov: nearly **200 million metric tons** of carbon emissions by 15 Nov 2026, **third-highest annual volume on record**; **~8.9 million hectares damaged (4.7% of Indonesia)**, worst fire season in years, driven by super-El Niño + land clearing.
- Worst hit: **South Papua**, then **West Kalimantan, Sumatra and East Nusa Tenggara** — fires on several islands, not one place.
- President Prabowo at the ASEAN summit, Manila, **15 Nov**: the big fires **"took nearly four months for us to control"** / "membutuhkan waktu hampir empat bulan untuk dikendalikan". He apologised to Malaysia and Singapore for the haze, saying "it's not our fault" — that clip went viral on Instagram (Big Alpha, 16 Nov, 12K likes; Greenpeace ID carousel, 17 Nov, 41K likes).
- Response: **71,000 personnel deployed, 16 officers died, 17 company concessions revoked, 104 companies investigated**. BNPB 19 Nov: **2,397 disasters in 2026**, karhutla the most at **1,096**, floods 647. Haze reached Malaysia and Singapore.
